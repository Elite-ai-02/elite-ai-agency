Straight Answers

Questions Owners Ask Us

The things people want to know before ordering — answered plainly.

Will it work with my customer's phone?
Yes. Every iPhone from 7 onward and almost every Android phone made in the last several years reads NFC cards natively. Nothing to install, nothing to switch on — the review page just opens.
Do I need to set up the Google review link myself?
No. When you order, we find your Google Business profile and link the card to your review page before it ships. If your business isn't on Google Maps yet, we'll help you set it up.
What if a customer doesn't want to leave a review?
Nothing happens — the page simply opens and they can close it. There's no pressure and no public trace. Most people who are already happy with you just needed the easy path to say so.
Is this against Google's rules?
Not at all. You're inviting real customers to leave honest reviews — the card only removes the friction of finding your page. We never filter, fake or gate reviews.
How long does delivery take, and what do I get?
Share your business details on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the format that suits you — counter card, table stand or bedside unit — along with pricing and delivery time for your city.
