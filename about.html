Our Story

Built To Solve Real Problems

Not a demo. Not a concept. Systems running in real businesses.

Elite AI & Smart Systems started with one simple observation: businesses lose money in the gap between a happy customer and a recorded review, and in the hours after closing when enquiries go unanswered.

EliteTap closes the first gap — a tap on a card opens your Google review page in one second. Our AI systems close the second — WhatsApp agents that qualify leads, answer questions and book appointments while you sleep.

We build for hotels, restaurants, cafés and ambitious local businesses across India, and we keep it practical: every system is judged by the revenue it returns, not by how clever it looks.
